# Code of Conduct

English · [Tiếng Việt](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.vi.md)

ColimaX adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The full text at that link applies to everyone who participates in the project: in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## In short

Expected:

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback, focused on the work rather than the person.
- Assume good faith and help newcomers.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting, the same channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not report conduct issues in a public issue. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that violate this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
